## Overview
The modern IT procurement process relies heavily on manual intervention for standard hardware requests. During our brainstorming sessions, the team identified that repetitive tasks—such as assigning configuration duties for standard laptops—create unnecessary bottlenecks. By leveraging ServiceNow’s capabilities, specifically Flow Designer and the Service Catalog, we conceptualized an automated pipeline. This pipeline eliminates the need for human triage by instantly routing approved laptop requests directly to the hardware team, ensuring faster deployment and reducing administrative overhead.
